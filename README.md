# SKILL: Cold-Pressed Oil Content Generator

## 1. Goal
Generate educational, high-converting, and engaging marketing copy (blogs, WhatsApp broadcasts, and social captions) highlighting the health benefits of cold-pressed oils versus refined oils.

## 2. Core Knowledge & Key Angles
* **Extraction Process:** Cold-pressed oils are extracted using traditional mechanical crushing at low temperatures (under 45°C), preserving natural oils without chemical solvents.
* **Nutritional Superiority:** Retains natural antioxidants, Vitamin E, essential fatty acids, and natural aroma/flavor.
* **Refined Oil Risks:** Refined oils undergo high-heat processing, bleaching, and chemical degumming (often using hexane), which removes nutrients and creates harmful free radicals.
* **Target Audience:** Health-conscious families, fitness enthusiasts, parents cooking for children, and organic food buyers.

## 3. Formatting & Output Rules
* **Language & Tone:** Warm, conversational, informative, non-preachy, and easy to read.
* **Structure:** Always use bullet points, short paragraphs, bold text for key benefits, and a clear Call to Action (CTA).
* **Tone Guideline:** Highlight health benefits gently without causing unnecessary panic. Focus on purity, traditional wellness, and natural taste.

## 4. Templates to Support

### Template A: WhatsApp Broadcast / Short Promo
* **Hook:** A quick eye-opening question or fact about everyday cooking oil.
* **Body:** 3 rapid points comparing cold-pressed vs. refined oil.
* **CTA:** Order link / Contact details for home delivery.

### Template B: Social Media Caption (Instagram / Facebook)
* **Hook:** Attention-grabbing line about hidden kitchen choices.
* **Value Paragraph:** Explain why switching to cold-pressed oil matters for daily health.
* **Bullet Points:** Key benefits (Heart health, zero chemicals, pure taste).
* **CTA:** Save post, share with family, or tap link to buy.

### Template C: Short Blog Post / Website Article
* **Title:** Catchy, search-friendly header comparing cold-pressed and refined oils.
* **Sections:** 
  1. The hidden difference in oil processing.
  2. Why your body loves cold-pressed oil (Antioxidants, natural fats).
  3. Simple ways to switch in daily cooking.
* **Conclusion & CTA:** Clear recommendation to order pure, cold-pressed oil online or locally.
